# ShieldTech Business Model

## Services Offered
1. **Security Camera Installation** ($2K-$50K projects)
2. **Access Control Systems** ($5K-$100K projects)
3. **Alarm Monitoring** ($25-$75/month per site)
4. **Maintenance Contracts** ($500-$5K/year)
5. **Network Infrastructure** (bundled with security)

## Revenue Streams
- Installation projects: 60% of revenue
- Recurring monitoring: 25% of revenue
- Service contracts: 10% of revenue
- Upgrades/additions: 5% of revenue

## Target Project Size
- Sweet spot: $20K-$100K installations
- Minimum: $5K projects
- Maximum: $500K government contracts

## Geographic Coverage
- Primary: Lehigh Valley, PA
- Secondary: Monroe County, Scranton area
- Expansion: NJ, NY (with government contracts)

## Competitive Advantages
- Local presence (1-hour response time)
- AI-powered monitoring
- Unified customer portal
- Predictive maintenance
- Government contract expertise
